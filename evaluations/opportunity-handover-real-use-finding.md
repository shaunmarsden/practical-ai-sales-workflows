# Opportunity Handover: Real-Use Finding

## Status

**Internal real-use evidence for the underlying method. Not independent validation, and not yet a test of the new public skill unchanged.**

Shaun used a private, repeatable AI-assisted handover method across 11 real sales-to-service handovers. A receiving colleague reported that the context was immediately useful and used one handover to challenge a material statement before relying on it. This is internal real-use evidence, not independent validation. The public skill is a sanitised reconstruction and has been tested only with fictional information.

No real handover documents, names, employers, links or identifying details are reproduced here. This finding describes what repeated private use supports and does not support, at a level general enough to publish.

## What Was Reviewed Privately

A private handover folder contained 11 real handover documents, created in three batches over time. The handovers covered both individual and multi-person accounts, including one substantially larger account. Representative documents from the earlier, middle and most recent batches were reviewed, not just the newest ones.

## What Repeated Use Supports

Across the documents reviewed, the handovers consistently carried useful sales context into the receiving team, including the current account position, people and confirmed roles, customer expectations and commitments, operational context relevant to the recipient, intended outcomes, possible measures and baselines, missing evidence, provisional project or next-step directions, source links, data and approval restrictions, and human ownership and review points.

Later handovers, from the more recent batches, showed stronger handling of missing information, conflicting records, non-standard commitments, provisional recommendations, source traceability, data restrictions and measurable outcomes than the earlier ones. This pattern, of the method improving with repeated real use, is itself part of the evidence: it suggests the earlier gaps were genuine and worth fixing, not just noise.

Shaun reports that he used a repeatable private Claude skill to create these handovers after deals closed. The public repository workflow was not necessarily run word for word against these deals. Treat this as real use of the underlying method, not as proof that the new public skill in this repository has already been used unchanged.

## Internal Recipient Feedback

An internal colleague who received one of the handovers described it as immediately valuable, asked whether the same approach was used for every deal, and said that having the context improved their confidence in later employer conversations. That colleague used the document to challenge a material delivery statement, which triggered a useful clarification before the statement was relied on.

The colleague is not named, and no detail about their employer, sector, programme or internal systems is included here. Their exact wording is not reproduced.

## What Changed in the Public Method

This finding prompted the addition of the reusable [opportunity-handover skill](../.agents/skills/opportunity-handover/SKILL.md) to this repository, alongside a harder fictional regression test built on [an update to the existing Hartwell scenario](../examples/hartwell-opportunity-handover-update.md). The public skill was written from general principles about what a good handover needs, not copied from the private material, and has only ever been run against fictional information.

## What the Evidence Does Not Support

- No measured time saving is available.
- No retention, revenue, conversion or productivity result can be attributed to the handovers.
- It is not known whether every handover was accepted without correction.
- It is not known whether every recommended action was completed.
- This is not independent external validation. The colleague who gave feedback is an internal colleague, not an external user of this repository.
- The new public skill has not yet been used unchanged in real work; only fictional information has tested it so far.
- No private documents, names, links or identifying details are published, and none should be inferred from what is written here.

## Privacy Boundary

Nothing in this finding names the colleague, their employer, the customer, any learner, sector, programme or internal system. No real document is redacted and published here; the public examples in this repository (Hartwell Analytics and its cast) remain entirely fictional and are not based on the private handovers.

## Next Evidence

The next useful step is testing the new public skill against a real or realistic handover directly, rather than relying only on the fictional regression test. After that, an independent salesperson trying the public skill on their own handover, and reporting where it helps or fails, is the next evidence beyond what the builder alone can provide.
