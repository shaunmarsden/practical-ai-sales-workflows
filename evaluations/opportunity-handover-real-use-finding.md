# Opportunity Handover: Real-Use Finding

## Status

**Internal real-use evidence for the underlying method. Not independent validation, and not yet a test of the new public skill unchanged.**

I used a private, repeatable handover method with AI on 11 real handovers from sales to service. A colleague who received one said the context was useful straight away, and used it to challenge an important statement before relying on it. That's internal evidence from real use, not independent validation. The public skill is a cleaned-up rebuild, and I've only tested it with made-up information.

I haven't reproduced any real handover document, name, employer, link or identifying detail. This page describes what repeated private use does and doesn't support, in terms general enough to publish.

## What Was Reviewed Privately

A private folder held 11 real handover documents, written in three batches over time. They covered accounts with one person and accounts with several, including one much larger account. I reviewed sample documents from the early, middle and latest batches, not just the newest.

## What Repeated Use Supports

Across the documents I reviewed, the handovers carried useful sales context to the receiving team every time. That included where the account stood, the people and their confirmed roles, what the customer expected and had been promised, working context the recipient needed, intended outcomes, possible measures and starting points, missing evidence, provisional next steps, source links, limits on data and approval, and who owned and reviewed what.

The later batches handled missing information, conflicting records, unusual commitments, provisional advice, tracing back to sources, data limits and measurable outcomes better than the early ones. That improvement over repeated real use is part of the evidence. It suggests the early gaps were real and worth fixing, not just noise.

I used a repeatable private Claude skill to write these handovers after deals closed. I didn't necessarily run this repository's public workflow word for word on these deals. Treat this as real use of the underlying method, not proof that the new public skill has been used unchanged.

## Internal Recipient Feedback

A colleague who received one of the handovers said it was useful straight away, asked whether I did the same for every deal, and said the context made them more confident in later conversations with the employer. They used the document to challenge an important statement about delivery, which led to a useful clarification before anyone relied on it.

I haven't named the colleague or given any detail about their employer, sector, programme or internal systems. I haven't reproduced their exact words.

## What Changed in the Public Method

This finding led me to add the reusable [opportunity-handover skill](../.agents/skills/opportunity-handover/SKILL.md) to this repository, along with a harder made-up regression test built on [an update to the existing Hartwell scenario](../examples/hartwell-opportunity-handover-update.md). I wrote the public skill from general principles about what a good handover needs. I didn't copy it from the private material, and it has only ever run on made-up information.

## What the Evidence Does Not Support

- There's no measured time saving.
- No retention, revenue, conversion or productivity result can be credited to the handovers.
- I don't know whether every handover was accepted without correction.
- I don't know whether every recommended action was done.
- This isn't independent validation. The colleague who gave feedback works with me. They aren't an outside user of this repository.
- Nobody has used the new public skill unchanged in real work yet. Only made-up information has tested it so far.
- I haven't published any private document, name, link or identifying detail, and none should be inferred from this page.

## Privacy Boundary

Nothing here names the colleague, their employer, the customer, any learner, sector, programme or internal system. I haven't published a redacted real document. The public examples in this repository, Hartwell Analytics and its cast, are entirely made up and aren't based on the private handovers.

## Next Evidence

The next step is to test the new public skill on a real or realistic handover, rather than relying only on the made-up regression test. After that, the next evidence beyond what I can provide alone would be an independent salesperson trying the public skill on their own handover and reporting where it helps or fails.
