# Fictional Objection Pattern Log: Second Test

> This log is entirely fictional, created to test whether the Review Objection Patterns skill can count distinct deals correctly and separate a real shared driver from repeated wording that hides different causes. Every company, person, objection and outcome is invented.

## Deliberate Test Points

- Entries 1 to 4 contain four objections but only three distinct deals. Linton Vale appears twice.
- The wording differs across entries 1 to 4, but the same current-state evidence gap sits underneath all four.
- Entries 5 to 7 sound similar because each says the change looks like a lot to roll out. Their drivers are different: timing and capacity, a technical unknown, and missing ownership.
- Entry 8 is important but isolated. One data residency question is not yet a recurring pattern.
- Outcomes support the diagnoses but do not prove them.
- The log is small and deliberately selected for testing. It is not representative of a wider pipeline.
