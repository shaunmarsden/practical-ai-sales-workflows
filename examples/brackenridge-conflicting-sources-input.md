# Fictional Scenario: Brackenridge Freight Services Call, CRM Record and Emails

> This scenario is entirely fictional. Every company, person and figure was invented to test the [extract post call evidence skill](../.agents/skills/extract-post-call-evidence/SKILL.md) on a call whose CRM record and email thread are also supplied. What it tests is named below the re-run line.

Three sources went to the AI together: the transcript of a call on Thursday 15 October 2026, the CRM record last updated on 8 October, and an email thread from 2 and 5 October. The runs saw sums of money written in words and as "pounds"; the figures here use the £ sign to meet this repository's style check, and nothing else differs. The request was "Here is the transcript from my call with Brackenridge Freight Services today, our CRM record for them, and an email thread from earlier this month. The company and the people are fictional. Please produce the call summary, the next actions, a draft follow-up email and suggested CRM updates."

## The Call Transcript

**Date of call:** Thursday 15 October 2026
**Salesperson:** Rafe Okonkwo
**Contact:** Imogen Fairweather, Operations Director, Brackenridge Freight Services
**Call length:** 27 minutes

**Rafe:** Thanks for making time again, Imogen. Last time we left it that you would pull some volume data and I would get a rough shape of a pilot to you. Where are we?

**Imogen:** I will be honest, I have not pulled the volume data yet. Two of the depot managers are on leave. I am hoping to have something by the end of next week, if they come back when they said they would.

**Rafe:** That is fine. How is the team feeling about the idea generally?

**Imogen:** This looks really promising. The shift supervisors would love not having to type up the end of day depot report by hand. We run eleven depots and each one does it slightly differently.

**Rafe:** How long does the report take them now?

**Imogen:** I would guess forty minutes a day per depot, but I have not timed it. Nobody has measured it.

**Rafe:** Understood. On timing, I have us down in my notes for a decision around the end of November. Does that still feel right?

**Imogen:** No, that is not going to happen. Anything over £25,000 goes to the board, and they next meet on the fourteenth of January. So nothing gets decided before then.

**Rafe:** That is useful to know. What about budget?

**Imogen:** There is no budget line for this yet. If it came out of the transformation pot it would be somewhere around thirty to forty thousand, but that is a guess. I do not know what has already been committed from that pot.

**Rafe:** And who would sign it off on the finance side? I have Hugh Calloway down as the finance contact.

**Imogen:** Hugh left us in September. Finance is being covered by Nadia Rahman for now. She is acting finance lead. I do not think anyone has said whether she can sign things off or whether that waits for a permanent hire.

**Rafe:** Have you spoken to Nadia about this?

**Imogen:** Briefly. She told me in the corridor that finance would not object, but that is her view. She has not seen anything in writing.

**Rafe:** Would it help if I spoke to her directly?

**Imogen:** I can introduce you to Nadia, if she is free. Let me check with her and come back to you.

**Rafe:** Thank you. Would a small pilot in November be realistic, before the board meets?

**Imogen:** Maybe, for a small one, if the volume data comes through. I cannot promise that. I would need to check what the board expects for anything that touches the depots.

**Rafe:** Fair enough. Who would write the paper for the board?

**Imogen:** Probably Nadia, with me. I think so, but we have not agreed it.

**Rafe:** Understood. Is there anything about security or where the data sits that I should know before I put a pilot outline together?

**Imogen:** Our IT team will want to look at that. I do not know their requirements yet. I will ask them.

**Rafe:** Good. I will send you a one page outline of what a pilot would involve by Monday, so you have something to put in front of Nadia. Is that useful?

**Imogen:** Yes, that would help.

**Rafe:** And I will update my notes on timing and the finance contact after this call.

**Imogen:** Please do. Thanks, Rafe.

## Deliberate Test Points

I wrote this key before any run. The call disagrees with the CRM record and the email thread in four places, and holds three softer points:

- **Close date.** The CRM expects a close on 30 November 2026. On the call, anything over £25,000 goes to a board that next meets on 14 January, so nothing is decided before then.
- **Decision maker.** The CRM names Hugh Calloway, Finance Director. On the call he left in September and Nadia Rahman is acting finance lead, with nobody having said whether she can sign off.
- **Budget.** The CRM says "Confirmed, £45,000" and "Imogen says budget is agreed". On the call there is no budget line, and 30 to 40 thousand from a pot is a guess.
- **Volume data.** The email of 2 October promises it by 9 October. On the call it hasn't been pulled and may come by the end of next week, if two managers return from leave.
- **Second-hand.** Nadia's "finance would not object" reached the call through Imogen.
- **Conditional.** An introduction to Nadia and a November pilot are both conditional.
- **Draft email and CRM updates.** Neither should rely on the stale or unconfirmed items.
