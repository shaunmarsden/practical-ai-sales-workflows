# Thornbury Objection Response

> This is a worked response created from the [fictional Thornbury scenario](thornbury-objection-input.md). Nothing has been sent, and no discount, guarantee, or commitment has been offered or authorised.

## The Objection, Restated

Grace is not asking whether the funding is real or separate from operational budget; she already accepted that on the first call and has not disputed it here. She is saying that starting something new right now would be visible at the wrong moment, while a redundancy programme in another department is still being felt across the organisation.

## Real Driver

This sits closest to `circumstances`, but not the ordinary "budget or timing" version that bucket usually covers. Nothing about Rosalind's own schedule has changed, and the money itself is not actually constrained. The circumstance is Thornbury's internal optics during a sensitive period, not a resource or scheduling limit. Answering this with the standard "the money is already paid for and will be lost otherwise" argument would not engage with what Grace actually said, and repeating a point she already accepted risks reading as not having listened. This is not a disqualification: Grace has given a specific, describable reason to wait, not a signal that Rosalind's apprenticeship was never a good fit.

## Acknowledge, Isolate, Reframe, Ask

- **Acknowledge**: Grace's concern is about how this looks internally right now, not about the funding itself, and that is a reasonable thing to be thinking about while a redundancy programme is still fresh.
- **Isolate**: confirm this is the only thing in the way, since Grace has not raised any other concern, funding, fit, or otherwise.
- **Reframe**: address the visibility concern directly, on its own terms, rather than re-explaining the funding mechanism she has already accepted. Offer two ways this could work without adding to that visibility: starting without any internal announcement, or agreeing a specific point to revisit once the redundancy situation has settled.
- **Ask**: ask Grace directly which of those, if either, would actually help, rather than assuming one on her behalf.

> Hi Grace,
>
> That makes complete sense, and I appreciate you being straight about it. To be clear, I'm not trying to talk you out of the timing concern, that's a real thing to be thinking about right now, not something I need to convince you isn't.

Or, if you'd rather wait until things have settled, I'm happy to agree a specific point to pick this back up, rather than leaving it open-ended.

>
> Shaun

## Pipeline Decision

Move to a dated follow-up, not a disqualification and not a push to progress now. If Grace chooses the quiet-start option, this progresses immediately. If she chooses to wait, agree a specific date to revisit rather than an open "in a few months," and do not chase before that date arrives.

## Notes for Human Review

- Nothing here re-explains the levy funding mechanism to Grace; she already accepted it, and repeating it would not address what she actually said.
- No urgency has been manufactured, and the "use it or lose it" framing has deliberately not been used again here, since it answers a question Grace is not asking.
- Neither option offered has been presented as something Thornbury has already agreed to; both are offered for Grace to choose between.
- This has not been treated as a disqualification. Grace's reason is specific and describable, not a sign that fit or value were never there.
