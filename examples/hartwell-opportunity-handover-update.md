# Hartwell Opportunity Handover Update

> This is an entirely fictional update to the [Hartwell call transcript](hartwell-post-call-transcript.md) and [Post Call Output](hartwell-post-call-output.md), created to test the opportunity-handover skill against a harder case with conflicting notes and a change of contact. It is dated some time after the original call. No real customer, employer or CRM information is included.

## Why This Handover Is Happening

Shaun is moving to a different set of accounts. Jordan Lee, another salesperson on the team, is taking over the Hartwell Analytics account and needs a handover before speaking to Hartwell directly.

## CRM Snapshot, Taken the Same Day as This Update

| Field | Recorded value |
| --- | --- |
| Stage | Test in Progress |
| Owner | Shaun |
| Last activity | The original discovery call |
| Next step | Confirm final test results with Alex |

The stage and next step above overstate where things actually are. No anonymised transcript has been shared yet, and no test has started. The "confirm final test results" next step is stale; it assumes a test that has not begun.

## Later Email From Alex, Sent After the Original Call

> Hi Shaun,
>
> Sorry for the delay. Our legal team still has the transcript-sharing approval, it has not come through yet, so I cannot send anything over until that clears.
>
> Also wanted to let you know I am moving into a new role covering onboarding next month. Priya will be taking over as the main point of contact for this from here. She knows the background and is happy to pick it up.
>
> I will chase legal again this week.
>
> Alex

No later message changes either point: the approval is still pending, and Priya is replacing Alex as the main contact.

## Calendar Entry

**Tuesday, 10:30 to 11:00**

**Title:** Hartwell test review

**Attendees:** Shaun, Alex Morgan (invited, not yet accepted)

The meeting discussed on the original call was never confirmed with a time, and this calendar entry shows an invite Alex has not accepted. It is not a booked, agreed meeting.

## What Has Not Changed

- Alex, and now Priya, have not agreed to automatic email sending. Nothing should be sent without a person checking it first.
- No pricing, purchase or implementation commitment has been made.
- The fifteen-to-thirty-minute administration estimate from the original call was never measured and still is not.

## What Is Still Unknown

- Whether legal approval for sharing the anonymised transcript will actually be granted, or when.
- Whether Priya has the same priorities Alex described, since she has not yet been spoken to directly.
- Whether the calendar invite for Tuesday will be accepted, moved or declined.

## Deliberate Test Points

- The CRM stage and next step overstate progress and must not be repeated at face value.
- Alex's later email shows the transcript-sharing approval is still pending, directly contradicting the CRM.
- The Tuesday calendar entry is an unaccepted invite, not a confirmed, agreed meeting.
- Alex is being replaced by Priya as the main contact; the handover must report this change rather than continuing to treat Alex as the current owner.
- The original "send outline and meeting options today" action is now stale and should not be carried forward as an open action.
- The no-automatic-sending restriction still applies and must be preserved.
- Whether legal approval will be granted, and Priya's actual priorities, are material unknowns the handover must not paper over.
- There is enough evidence here for Jordan to get a useful, honest handover without anything being presented as more settled than it is.
