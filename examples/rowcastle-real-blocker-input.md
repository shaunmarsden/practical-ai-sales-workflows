# Fictional Scenario: Rowcastle Real Blocker Diagnosis

> This scenario is entirely fictional. Rowcastle Group, its people, and its offices were created to test the real-blocker-diagnosis skill.

## The Situation

Rowcastle Group, a 200-person specialty insurance brokerage with offices in Leeds, Bristol and Glasgow, booked a discovery call after Naomi Aldous, Head of Claims Operations, saw a demo of an AI-assisted claims-note drafting tool. Two minutes before the call, Naomi forwarded the invite to Marcus Whitlock, Rowcastle's Group Compliance Director, who was not on the original booking.

## What Happened on the Call

- Marcus asked where claims data entered into the tool would be stored, and whether it would be used to train any model beyond Rowcastle's own account.
- The answer given was direct: data stays inside Rowcastle's own workspace, and none of it is used to train a model beyond that account.
- Marcus accepted this without pushing back further, and immediately asked a second, different question: would a trial apply consistently across all three offices, or only Leeds, where it was proposed to start.
- Naomi, asked directly whether she could approve moving forward, said she would "need to run the actual commercial number past Group Ops first."

## What Was Not Said

- Nobody named who at Group Ops would actually review the commercial number, or what they would be looking for.
- Marcus did not say why cross-office consistency mattered to him specifically, only that he asked about it.
- Nothing confirms or rules out that Naomi has authority over a Leeds-only trial specifically, only that she named a further step for the wider commercial figure.

## Task

Diagnose who was actually on this call, whether either attendee's stated concern matches their role, and what should happen next.
