## Prepared Meeting Brief

### Objective

Leave with the decision path, the right people for the next conversation and agreement on whether Maya wants the one-page example before or after the 2nd December review.

### Opening

> You asked to keep this away from a formal proposal until after the budget review, so I have kept today's conversation focused on the people, decision process and one relevant example.

### Questions

1. Who besides you needs to believe the reporting problem is worth fixing?
2. Who can approve budget, and what will they need to see?
3. Does the 2nd December review decide whether this progresses, or only whether it receives further investigation?
4. Are approved exports available from the four source systems, or is that still unknown?
5. What would make the Bramfield example useful rather than simply interesting?
6. What should the next agreed step be after the budget review, and who owns it?

### Proof Point

Use the Bramfield weekly operations reporting example:

- preparation fell from around seven hours to three hours a week in the fictional six-week pilot;
- it used approved exports, not a live production integration;
- a manager reviewed exceptions before sharing the report;
- present it as an example of the method, not a forecast for Alderwick.

### Close

> Based on what we have covered, who should join the next conversation, and should we agree that meeting now or wait until the budget review has happened?
