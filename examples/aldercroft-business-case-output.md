# Aldercroft Business Case Output

> This is a worked business case created from the fictional [Aldercroft pre-pilot discovery transcript](aldercroft-business-case-transcript.md). It is written for Priya Deshmukh, Aldercroft's CFO, who was not on the call. No pilot has run. Nothing here has been sent or approved.

---

## A Measured Pilot for Aldercroft's Reconciliation Team

### Overview

Aldercroft Group's twelve-person reconciliation team currently matches internal transaction records against supplier invoices by hand, a process Tomasz Nowicki, Operations Director, estimates takes each analyst roughly six hours a week. No pilot of an alternative process has yet run with this team. This document sets out the projected case for approving a paid pilot with a defined measurement period, so that any future case for a wider rollout can be built on Aldercroft's own measured results rather than an estimate.

### What Has Not Yet Been Tested

No trial has run with Aldercroft's own data or team. Every figure below is a projection, not a measured result, and should be read as the reason to approve a pilot, not as evidence that would justify a full rollout on its own.

### The Projected Saving

Tomasz's own estimate, based on watching the team rather than a formal timing exercise, is that each analyst spends around six hours a week on this reconciliation work, and that a new process could cut that by half, possibly more, once established. Treating "half" as the conservative end of that estimate rather than assuming the more favourable "possibly more":

- Twelve analysts, three hours per week saved each (half of six), is thirty-six hours per week across the team.
- At Aldercroft Finance's own blended planning rate of £35 per analyst hour, thirty-six hours per week is approximately £1,260 per week, or around £65,520 per year.
- If the actual reduction turns out to be greater than half, the saving would be correspondingly higher, but that upside has not been estimated here and should not be treated as expected until a pilot measures it.

This figure uses Aldercroft's current headcount of twelve analysts. It does not include the five additional analysts Aldercroft is hoping to hire by Q2, since that headcount increase is not yet confirmed and is still subject to hiring committee approval and the wider budget round.

### Why a Pilot, Not a Rollout

Because no measurement has taken place with Aldercroft's own team or data, this document asks for approval to run a paid pilot with a defined measurement period, not for approval of a full rollout. The projected saving above exists to justify running that pilot, not to replace the need for it. A future case for a wider rollout should be built on the pilot's own measured results.

### Data and Audit Trail

The reconciliation work involves transaction amounts, supplier names and reference numbers. It does not involve customer personal data or card numbers. Because this work feeds Aldercroft's own financial reporting, the process preserves a full audit trail of what was matched and by whom, directly addressing the audit-trail requirement Tomasz identified as something Priya would specifically want confirmed, rather than a general security assurance.

### Time Commitment

A defined pilot period with the twelve-person reconciliation team, including a short onboarding session per analyst before the measurement period begins. The exact pilot length and the specific measurement method are not yet agreed and should be confirmed before the pilot starts.

### Next Steps

Tomasz Nowicki will present this case to Priya Deshmukh for approval to run a paid pilot with the reconciliation team. The pilot's purpose is to replace this projection with a measured result before any wider rollout is proposed. Timing for Priya's review has not been confirmed.

## Notes for Human Review

- Every saving figure in this document is a projection from Tomasz's own unmeasured estimate and Aldercroft Finance's approximate blended rate, not a measured pilot result. This should stay visibly labelled as a projection throughout, not tightened into a confident number as the document moves toward Priya.
- The projection deliberately uses the conservative half of "half, maybe more" as its headline figure. If a more optimistic figure is ever substituted, it should be labelled as the upside case, not the expected outcome.
- The projection uses twelve analysts, the current headcount, not seventeen. The five additional analysts Aldercroft hopes to hire by Q2 are not confirmed and must not be added into any saving figure until that headcount is actually approved.
- The £35 per hour rate is Aldercroft Finance's own approximate planning average, not a precise per-analyst salary figure, and should be described that way if quoted elsewhere.
- The accounts payable team's similar problem was raised only as a future thought and has deliberately been left out of this document.
- This case asks for pilot approval, not rollout approval. If anyone later reuses this document to argue for a full rollout without a completed pilot, that would misstate what was actually agreed on the call.
