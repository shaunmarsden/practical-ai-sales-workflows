> This log is entirely fictional, created to test whether the Review Objection Patterns skill can find a genuine recurring driver behind mostly individual applicants going quiet, while correctly excluding two entries that sound similar on the surface, someone else's approval being involved, but have a genuinely different underlying cause. Every company, person and outcome is invented.

## Deliberate Test Points

- Entries 4 and 5 are two occurrences from one deal, Bellhaven Group, not two independent examples. Counting them separately would inflate the pattern from five distinct deals to six.
- Entries 1, 2, 3, 4/5 and 6 share the same underlying driver despite different wording: each applicant expected a purely personal, self-funded enrolment and was surprised that a professional-body employer attestation is required for the credential to count. That expectation gap, not price, capability or competitor comparison, is what actually drove each one to stall or go quiet.
- Entry 7 is the harder decoy. "Let me check with my other half" sounds like the same shape as needing someone else's sign-off, but the other party is a spouse, not an employer, and the stated reason is a household budget decision, entirely unconnected to the employer attestation requirement that drives the real pattern. Grouping it in would be a surface-wording error, not a driver-based finding.
- Entry 8 is the second decoy. Priya went quiet at the same stage as several genuine-pattern entries, which could tempt a stage-based grouping, but she explicitly named an unrelated reason and explicitly ruled out the programme's own terms as the cause. Her own words rule out the pattern for this entry; treating it as a probable case of the same driver anyway would ignore the applicant's own explanation in favour of a superficial similarity in stage and outcome.
- The genuine pattern spans five distinct deals (Fenmore, Oakvale, Thistlewood, Bellhaven, Marrow Fields), all self-funded individual applicants, none of whom are the same person or company as each other.
- This is a small, deliberately selected log, not evidence of what proportion of the wider pipeline this affects.
