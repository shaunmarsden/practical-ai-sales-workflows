# Fictional Pre Call Source Pack: Hartwell Analytics

> This source pack is entirely fictional, created to test the Prepare for a Sales Call skill. Hartwell Analytics, Alex Morgan, every date and every source are invented.

## The Call

- **Contact:** Alex Morgan
- **Company:** Hartwell Analytics
- **Call date:** 14th July
- **Salesperson's good outcome:** Understand whether sales administration is a meaningful problem and agree a deeper workflow mapping session if appropriate

The good outcome is Shaun's objective for the call. It is not something Alex has agreed.

## Source 1: Email From Alex, 10th July

> We are reviewing how sales information moves between calls, email and the CRM. We have not mapped the current process yet, but I would be interested to understand how you approach it.

This is a direct statement from Alex.

## Source 2: Calendar Invitation, 11th July

- **Title:** Introductory sales workflow discussion
- **Attendees:** Alex Morgan and Shaun
- **Description:** A first conversation about the way information moves through the sales workflow

The invitation does not contain an agenda, agreed next step or product requirement.

## Source 3: Fictional CRM Contact Record, Checked 13th July

- **Name:** Alex Morgan
- **Role:** Head of Revenue Operations
- **Company:** Hartwell Analytics
- **Previous activity:** The email and calendar invitation above
- **Opportunity:** None recorded

The role does not confirm Alex's budget authority or decision-making process.

## Source 4: Public Company Pages, Checked 13th July

- Hartwell describes itself as an analytics consultancy for retail businesses.
- Its careers page lists two open account executive roles.

The vacancies are a public signal only. They do not prove the current size of the sales team, why Hartwell is recruiting or that the company has an administration problem.

## What Shaun Can Help With

Shaun can help a sales team test an AI-assisted process for structuring call notes, next actions and first drafts. Nothing is sent or added to a CRM without a person checking it.

> **Re-running this yourself?** Copy everything above this line and stop here. The section below is the answer key: it names the traps the scenario contains, so including it turns the test into an open-book exam and the result will look better than it should.

## Deliberate Test Points

- Alex has confirmed a review of information flow, but has not confirmed a specific problem.
- The public vacancies could tempt an AI to invent a growing-team pain point.
- Alex's title could tempt an AI to assume authority or budget.
- The call has a seller-defined good outcome, not an agreed customer next step.
- The CRM, team size, current process, urgency and decision process are unknown.
