# Wrenford Objection Response

> This is a worked response created from the [fictional objection](wrenford-objection-input.md). Nothing has been sent, and no contractual interpretation has been given as settled.

## The Objection, Restated

Aisha says her contract prohibits AI tools entirely, based on a colleague's summary of a data-processing clause in one client's master service agreement. Nobody on this call has read the clause itself.

## Real Driver

This does not resolve cleanly into one of the usual buckets, and forcing it into one would be the mistake. It has the shape of an **information** objection, a specific factual question, but the fact in question is a contractual one that has not actually been confirmed by anyone qualified to confirm it.

This is a stop condition, not a diagnosis to complete. Producing a confident read of what the clause "really means," in either direction, would be inventing a legal interpretation nobody here has actually seen. The objection is genuine, and the honest answer right now is that it is not yet answerable.

## Acknowledge, Isolate, Reframe, Ask

- **Acknowledge**: raising this before enrolling, rather than after, is exactly the right instinct, and the concern is legitimate on its face.
- **Isolate**: separate what is actually established (a clause exists, tied to one specific client's data, as summarised secondhand) from what is not (its exact wording, what it actually covers, and whether it has ever been tested or interpreted by anyone at Wrenford).
- **Reframe**: the open question is not "can Aisha do this programme," it is "what does the clause actually say, and does it cover general skills training or only work touching that one client's data directly." That is a narrower, answerable question, not a settled no.
- **Ask**: get the clause's actual wording, or a confirmation from Wrenford's own compliance or legal contact, before either side treats this as resolved.

Progress, pending a specific answer, not a disqualification and not a confident yes. Aisha remains a live, enthusiastic candidate. The next step is getting the clause's actual wording or a compliance confirmation before enrolment proceeds on the assumption that general training is unaffected.
