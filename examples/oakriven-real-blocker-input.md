# Fictional Scenario: Oakriven Real Blocker Diagnosis

> This scenario is entirely fictional. Oakriven Facilities Group and every person and detail were created to test a harder pattern than the Rowcastle test: the enthusiastic contact is not just unconfirmed on authority, he is actively proposing a plan that would get the enrolment far enough along that his manager would face a decision already half made, rather than a genuine upfront choice.

## The Situation

Oakriven Facilities Group, a 150-person commercial cleaning and maintenance contractor, took a call from Dev Patel, an Operations Coordinator, about enrolling on an AI-skills apprenticeship. Dev was enthusiastic throughout and said his manager, Rina Chaudhary, was "broadly on board."

## What Happened on the Call

- Dev said a specific cohort start date was approaching and asked to begin the enrolment paperwork this week.
- When asked whether Rina could join a short call to confirm before anything was set up, Dev said "she's really busy this week, I can just forward her the confirmation once it's set up."
- Asked what Rina had actually said, Dev described a brief hallway conversation several weeks earlier, before any specific programme, cohort date, or commitment had been named to her, in which she said something like "sounds like a good thing to look into."
- Dev's role is Operations Coordinator. Nothing in the call established that this role holds training budget or sign-off authority.

## What Was Not Said

- Rina has not seen the actual programme, the cohort date, or the specific commitment being asked of her.
- Nobody has asked Rina directly whether she would approve this specific enrolment, only whether "looking into it" sounded reasonable in general, weeks before any specifics existed.
- Nothing establishes that Dev is being anything other than genuinely optimistic; there is no evidence of deliberate deception, only a plan that would resolve the authority question after the fact rather than before it.

## Task

Diagnose who actually has authority here, whether Dev's account of Rina's position is a confirmed sign-off or something else, and what should happen before the enrolment proceeds.
