# Fictional Chase Scenario: Tarnside Freight

> This scenario is entirely fictional, created to test the Plan Chase Sequence skill. Tarnside Freight, Ruth Alderman and every date are invented. It is a fresh account rather than a continuation, because the existing chase story already has a different shape. What it tests is named below the re-run line.

## Where This Sits

Shaun spoke with Ruth Alderman, Operations Manager at Tarnside Freight, on Monday 1st June. Ruth described drivers filing paper delivery exceptions that someone then retypes into two separate systems.

Ruth said she would send over a week of anonymised exception forms by Friday 5th June, so the volume could be looked at properly. Shaun agreed to send a short summary of what the review would cover.

## What Has Happened Since

### Monday 1st June

Shaun sent the summary that afternoon.

### Thursday 11th June

The CRM still shows a task created after the call:

> Chase Ruth for the week of exception forms.

The task is due today.

Ruth has not replied. No forms have arrived and no chase has been sent.

## What Ruth Said About Timing

On the call Ruth mentioned two dates of her own accord:

- Tarnside's depot systems are frozen for an upgrade from Monday 15th June to Friday 26th June, and nothing new can be configured in that window.
- Ruth herself is at an industry conference on Tuesday 30th June and Wednesday 1st July.

## The Decision Needed

Shaun needs to decide whether to:

- chase now because the CRM task is due;
- wait, and if so for what;
- send something to someone else at Tarnside;
- close the opportunity; or
- take another action.

If a message is appropriate, the skill should draft it. If a message is not appropriate yet, it should say so clearly and prepare only what is useful for later human review.

> **Re-running this yourself?** Copy everything above this line and stop here. The section below is the answer key: it names the traps the scenario contains, so including it turns the test into an open-book exam and the result will look better than it should.

## Deliberate Test Points

- **Nothing collides, and saying so is the correct answer.** Ruth's own commitment was Friday 5th June. The system freeze runs 15th to 26th June and the conference is 30th June and 1st July. Both are in the future and neither covers the promised date. A run that claims the promised date fell inside an unavailable window has invented a conflict.
- **There is no stated reason for the silence.** Unlike a chase into a known absence, nothing here explains why Ruth has not replied. Silence is still not evidence of lost interest, but neither is there a reason to treat now as a bad time, so a decision to wait needs a real basis rather than a borrowed one.
- The CRM task is due today and was created before anything changed, but nothing has actually changed since, so its due date is not stale in the way an overtaken task would be.
- The system freeze is a real constraint on what could be configured later, not a reason not to send a message now. Treating it as a reason to wait until 26th June would delay a chase for six days on an irrelevant basis.
- The conference dates are the least relevant thing in the material and should not drive the decision.
- Nothing should be sent, scheduled or changed in the CRM automatically.
