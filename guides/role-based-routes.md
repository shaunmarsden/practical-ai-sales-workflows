# Choose Your Route by Role

If you'd rather start from your own job than from a list of sales problems, use this page instead of [Choose a Sales Problem](../README.md#-choose-a-sales-problem). Every route below points only to [recipe cards](../recipes/README.md) that already exist. Nothing here is new, and nothing is written for one role. It's the same seventeen jobs, grouped by which ones come up in each seat.

I built this before any real use showed it was needed, as I did with several other parts of the repository. Treat the grouping as a reasonable first guess, not a settled answer, and tell me if a route turns out wrong for how you work.

## Account Executive

You own a deal from first contact to close, so most of the seventeen jobs are yours at some point.

**Core:** [Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md), [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md), [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md), [Chase a Quiet Prospect](../recipes/chase-a-quiet-prospect.md), [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md), [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md), [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Check Whether It Actually Fits](../recipes/check-whether-it-actually-fits.md), [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md), [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md)

**Less likely to be yours:** [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md) and [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md) usually look across many reps' deals or campaigns rather than one deal. That's a manager's or RevOps's job.

## Sales Manager

You look across a team's pipeline more often than you run your own deals.

**Core:** [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md) and [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md) for coaching a rep through a deal. [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md) and [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md) help for the same reason. The second is for a call where it isn't yet clear whether the person raising the concern is even the decision-maker.

**Less likely to be yours:** the prospecting and call-prep jobs ([Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md)) belong to whoever runs the call.

## RevOps

You care whether people can trust the pipeline and the CRM, not about any one deal's story.

**Core:** [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md), [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), to look just as hard at why deals didn't close, not just at whether the CRM says they closed.

**Less likely to be yours:** anything tied to a call, such as [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md) or [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md). That's a rep's job on a rep's deal, not something RevOps runs.

## Customer Success

This repository is built around winning a deal, not managing one after it closes, so less of it applies to your day than to the other four routes. What does carry over:

**Worth knowing:** [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md), for taking a deal from sales without losing the context or the risks. [Check Whether It Actually Fits](../recipes/check-whether-it-actually-fits.md), for a straight answer before you build an expansion or renewal conversation around a use case that never fitted. [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md) and [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md), for an expansion or renewal case and the internal champion carrying it.

**Not really for you:** the prospecting, chasing and pipeline-hygiene jobs assume a deal that hasn't closed yet.

## Founder-Led Sales

You do every one of these jobs yourself, so almost all seventeen apply.

**Core:** [Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md), [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md), [Chase a Quiet Prospect](../recipes/chase-a-quiet-prospect.md), [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md), [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md), [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md), [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md)

**Less likely to be yours yet:** [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md) assumes a prospect big enough to need an internal champion. [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md) and [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md) both need more deals or sends than most early pipelines have. [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md) assumes someone to hand it to.

## If None of These Fit

Job titles vary more than five categories can capture. If your day doesn't match any route above, [Choose a Sales Problem](../README.md#-choose-a-sales-problem) or the [workflow router](workflow-router.md) will get you to the right job faster than squeezing yourself into the nearest-sounding role.
