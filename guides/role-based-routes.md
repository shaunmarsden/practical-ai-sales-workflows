# Choose Your Route by Role

If you would rather start from your own job than from a list of sales problems, use this instead of [Choose a Sales Problem](../README.md#-choose-a-sales-problem). Every route below only points to [recipe cards](../recipes/README.md) that already exist. Nothing here is new content, and nothing is written specifically for one role; it is the same seventeen jobs, grouped by which ones actually come up in each seat.

Built ahead of real usage evidence showing this was needed, the same way several other verticals were built ahead of their own real-test bar. Treat the grouping below as a reasonable starting guess, not a settled answer, and say so if a route turns out to be wrong for how you actually work.

## Account Executive

You own a deal from first contact to close, so most of the seventeen jobs are yours at some point.

**Core:** [Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md), [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md), [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md), [Chase a Quiet Prospect](../recipes/chase-a-quiet-prospect.md), [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md), [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md), [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Check Whether It Actually Fits](../recipes/check-whether-it-actually-fits.md), [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md), [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md)

**Less likely to be yours:** [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md) and [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md) are usually a manager's or RevOps's view across many reps' deals or campaigns, not one deal at a time.

## Sales Manager

You are looking across a team's pipeline more often than running your own deals.

**Core:** [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md) and [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md) for coaching a rep through a specific deal; [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md) and [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md) for the same reason, the second for a call where it is not yet clear whether the person raising the concern is even the decision-maker.

**Less likely to be yours:** the prospecting and call-prep jobs ([Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md)) belong to whoever is actually running the call.

## RevOps

You care about whether the pipeline and the CRM can be trusted, not about any one deal's story.

**Core:** [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md), [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md), [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md)

**Worth knowing:** [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), for the same honest-review discipline applied to why deals actually did not close, not just whether the CRM says so.

**Less likely to be yours:** anything call-specific, [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md), [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md), is a rep's job on a rep's deal, not something RevOps runs.

## Customer Success

Worth being honest here: this repository is built around winning a deal, not managing one after it closes, so less of it applies to your day-to-day than the other four routes. What does still transfer:

**Worth knowing:** [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md), for receiving a deal from sales without losing the context or the risks; [Check Whether It Actually Fits](../recipes/check-whether-it-actually-fits.md), for an honest read before an expansion or renewal conversation gets built around a use case that never actually fit; [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md) and [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md), for an expansion or renewal case and the internal champion carrying it.

**Not really for you:** the prospecting, chasing and pipeline-hygiene jobs assume a deal that has not closed yet.

## Founder-Led Sales

You are doing every one of these jobs yourself, so almost all seventeen apply.

**Core:** [Find the Next Prospect](../recipes/find-the-next-prospect.md), [Prepare for a Sales Call](../recipes/prepare-for-a-sales-call.md), [Follow Up After a Sales Call](../recipes/follow-up-after-a-sales-call.md), [Build a Business Case](../recipes/build-a-business-case.md), [Chase a Quiet Prospect](../recipes/chase-a-quiet-prospect.md), [Handle an Objection](../recipes/handle-an-objection.md), [Spot the Real Blocker](../recipes/spot-the-real-blocker.md), [Move a Stalled Decision](../recipes/move-a-stalled-decision.md), [Review a Lost Opportunity](../recipes/review-a-lost-opportunity.md), [Review Your Pipeline](../recipes/review-your-pipeline.md), [Keep Your CRM Honest](../recipes/keep-your-crm-honest.md), [Get a Weekly View](../recipes/get-a-weekly-view.md)

**Less likely to be yours yet:** [Brief Your Champion](../recipes/brief-your-champion.md) assumes an internal champion at a prospect complex enough to need one; [Spot a Real Objection Pattern](../recipes/spot-a-real-objection-pattern.md) and [Review an Outbound Campaign](../recipes/review-an-outbound-campaign.md) both need more deal or send volume than most early-stage pipelines have; [Hand Over an Opportunity](../recipes/hand-over-an-opportunity.md) assumes someone to hand it to.

## If None of These Fit

Job titles vary more than these five categories can capture. If your actual day does not match any route above, [Choose a Sales Problem](../README.md#-choose-a-sales-problem) or the [workflow router](workflow-router.md) will get you to the right one faster than forcing yourself into the closest-sounding role.
