# Output Template: Champion Enablement Package

> Instructions: the AI must output the package using this exact Markdown structure.

## 1. Decision Summary

- **The Ask:** [What the champion is asking the audience to agree to or approve]
- **The Evidence Behind It:** [The one or two points from the case most likely to matter to this specific audience]
- **What Is Still Outstanding:** [Anything unconfirmed, stated plainly, not smoothed over]

## 2. Role-Specific Evidence

For each further stakeholder the champion will present to or forward this to:

- **Stakeholder and Setting:** [Name, role, and how they will receive this, a meeting, a forwarded note]
- **What Is Actually Known About Their Concern:** [Grounded in something they or the champion said, or "not established beyond their role" if that is genuinely the case]
- **Relevant Evidence for This Concern:** [Only the evidence that speaks to what is actually known, not a repeated generic pitch]

## 3. Likely Questions and Honest Answers

- **Question:** [A question the evidence itself suggests will come up]
- **Answer:** [An honest answer, including "not yet confirmed" where that is genuinely the answer]

## 4. Internal Note (If the Situation Calls for One)

- **From:** [The champion, in their own name]
- **To:** [The specific further stakeholder]
- **Draft:** [Written for the champion to send themselves, never implying the seller is contacting this person directly]

## 5. What Still Requires a Person

- **The Champion Must Confirm:** [That every claim here is one they are actually prepared to stand behind]
- **The Seller Must Confirm:** [That nothing outstanding has been upgraded to settled anywhere in this package]
- **Sending or Presenting:** [Stays with the champion, not this output]
