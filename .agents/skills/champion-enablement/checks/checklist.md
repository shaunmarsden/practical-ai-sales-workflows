# Human Review Checklist

> Before the champion uses this package, the champion and the seller supporting them must verify the following.

## Factual Accuracy

- [ ] Does every stakeholder's evidence section trace to something actually said, not to an inference from their job title?
- [ ] Has any pending confirmation, unconfirmed figure, or unanswered question been quietly upgraded to a settled fact anywhere in the package?

## Commercial Judgement

- [ ] Is the champion actually prepared to stand behind every claim in this package in the room?
- [ ] Does the decision summary lead with what will actually matter to this specific audience, not a generic case restated?

## Completeness

- [ ] Has a champion actually agreed to carry this internally, rather than this being prepared speculatively?
- [ ] Is every further stakeholder covered here someone the champion is actually presenting to or forwarding this to, not someone assumed to be relevant?

## Final Approval

- [ ] I approve this package, the internal note if included, and the champion's plan to present or send it.
- [ ] I reject this package. If rejected, note why in the CRM or notes.
