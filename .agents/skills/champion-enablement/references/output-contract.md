# Output Contract: Champion Enablement

This contract defines the strict boundaries for this skill. The AI must adhere to these rules without exception.

## What the AI MUST Do

- **Ground every stakeholder section in something actually said.** A further stakeholder's evidence section must trace to a specific statement from them, the champion, or the call record, not to a plausible-sounding inference from their job title.
- **Label every outstanding item as outstanding.** A pending confirmation, an unconfirmed figure, or an unanswered question stays labelled that way through every section of the package, including the decision summary and the internal note.
- **Address the champion only.** Every deliverable is material for the champion to use, send, or present themselves, addressed to or written for the champion, never to a further stakeholder directly.

## What the AI MUST NOT Do

- **No priority invented from a title.** If nothing is actually known about a further stakeholder's concern beyond their role, say so plainly rather than filling the gap with what the title suggests.
- **No settled facts from open items.** Never resolve a pending confirmation, an unconfirmed figure, or an unanswered question into a settled fact to make the champion's case look stronger than the evidence supports.
- **No direct contact with anyone beyond the champion.** The AI does not draft something addressed to a further stakeholder as though the seller were contacting them directly, and does not treat the champion as a formality to route around.
- **No invented likely questions.** A question anticipated for the champion to prepare for must come from what the evidence itself leaves open, not from a generic list of objections that could apply to any deal.
